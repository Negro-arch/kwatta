About KWATTA

We are building the trust layer for second-hand trading in Bambili and Bamenda.

KWATTA is a peer-to-peer marketplace built for students at Bambili campus and residents of Bamenda. Every user is both buyer and seller. Every deal is tracked, verified and protected.

The problem

Buying second-hand is already normal. Trusting a stranger isn't.

Students move in and out of Bambili every semester. They need to buy cheaply and sell quickly — but the current options (Facebook groups, WhatsApp statuses, word of mouth) offer no protection, no verification and no dispute process. KWATTA exists to fix that.

🎯

Hyper-local by design

Every listing is tagged to a real neighborhood — Bambili campus, Mile 4, Mile 6, Nkwen, Mankon, Up Station. You know exactly how far you'll walk before you commit.

🛡️

Built around trust

Email confirmation + national ID verification unlock a public badge. Sellers carry their reputation with them — good behaviour is rewarded, bad behaviour is visible.

💬

Negotiation, made official

Haggling is part of the culture. KWATTA turns it into a tracked offer system so the agreed price is locked into the order — no more "you said X" arguments.

Our principles

What we believe

🤝

Community first

KWATTA is not a shop. It's a community marketplace. The same profile lets you post what you want to sell and buy what you need.

🔍

Transparency

One small commission on completed orders, shown before you confirm. No subscription. No listing fee. Nothing charged if the order never completes.

🌍

Bilingual, no exception

Cameroon is bilingual. KWATTA is bilingual. The entire interface switches between English and French with one tap.

Our values

Safety, Fairness, Simplicity

Three non-negotiable principles that shape every feature we build.

🛡️

Safety

Money held until receipt is confirmed. IDs verified by hand. Reports handled by real people.

⚖️

Fairness

Same rules for buyers and sellers. Both sides rate each other. Commission split transparently.

⚡

Simplicity

List in under 2 minutes. Pay in one tap. Withdraw in one request. No jargon, no hidden steps.

Where we're going

The roadmap

📍

Now — Bambili & Bamenda

Launching with the University of Bamenda's Bambili campus and the surrounding neighborhoods. Building the trust infrastructure that the rest of the country will use.

🚀

Next — North West & West

Extending to Buea, Bafoussam and other university towns. Same playbook, same safety principles, same bilingual interface.
