What KWATTA is

One account. You are both buyer and seller.

KWATTA is not a shop — it is a community marketplace. The same profile lets you post what you want to sell and buy what you need, with the tools to do it safely.

Publish a listing in minutes

Add photos, a clear description, your price and a precise neighborhood location — Bambili campus, Mile 4, Mile 6, Nkwen, Mankon and more. Buyers know exactly where the item is.

Browse and search with real filters

Filter by category, price range, condition, neighborhood and distance. Sort by newest, cheapest or closest — so you find the right item without scrolling for hours.

Negotiate with a built-in offer system

Haggling is part of the culture — so KWATTA makes it official. Send an offer, the seller accepts, counters or declines, and the agreed price is locked into the order.

Private chat, no phone numbers

Ask questions, share extra photos and agree on a handover point inside KWATTA. Your number stays private until you decide to share it.

Follow sellers you trust

Follow the classmates and vendors who always deliver. Their new listings appear in your feed first, so you get the good deals before anyone else.

A wallet that holds your money

Top up your KWATTA wallet, pay for orders instantly and withdraw your earnings to MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money whenever you want.

How it works

From listing to payout in five clear steps

Every order follows the same protected path. Money is held safely until the buyer confirms the item arrived as described.

1

Seller lists the item

Photos, description, price and neighborhood. The listing goes live immediately and reaches buyers nearby.

2

Buyer pays into KWATTA

The buyer accepts the offer and pays with the KWATTA wallet, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money or cash. KWATTA holds the funds.

3

Seller ships or hands over

The seller is notified and sends the item — by campus handover, moto delivery or a meeting point you both agree on.

4

Buyer confirms receipt

The buyer inspects the item and confirms it matches the listing. If something is wrong, they can open a report before confirming.

5

Funds released to the seller

The money lands in the seller's KWATTA wallet, ready to withdraw. Both sides can then rate each other.

Transparent commission. KWATTA takes a small commission on both sides of a completed order — a modest buyer service fee and a modest seller fee, shown before you confirm. No subscription, no listing fee, and nothing is charged if the order never completes.

Trust & safety

Built so a stranger on campus feels safe

Buying second-hand only works when both sides trust the deal. KWATTA verifies people, holds the money and keeps a record of everything.

Two-step verification

Confirm your email, then upload your national ID. Our admins validate it by hand — and only then does the verified badge appear on your profile.

Money held until you confirm

The buyer's payment sits with KWATTA, not with the seller, until the item is received as described. That is what makes paying a stranger reasonable.

Reviews with star ratings

After every completed order both sides rate each other. A seller's score is public, so good behaviour is rewarded and bad behaviour is visible.

Real-time notifications

New offer, accepted counter-offer, payment received, item shipped, message waiting — you are told the moment it happens, not hours later.

Boost requests to get seen

Need to sell fast before you travel home? Request a boost and admins push your listing to the top of the feed and the homepage for a set period.

For sellers & admins

A serious back office behind a simple app

Sellers get a dashboard for their listings, orders and earnings. Admins get full control of the marketplace — because a community platform lives or dies on moderation.

User management

Admins see every account, assign roles and act on abuse immediately.

  • Assign roles: student, seller, moderator, admin
  • Ban or suspend accounts that break the rules
  • Validate national IDs and grant verified badges

Listing moderation

Every listing can be reviewed before or after it goes live, with a full action set.

  • Approve, suspend or delete listings
  • Verify listings so buyers see a trust mark
  • Handle boost requests and promote listings

Transactions & withdrawals

Full visibility of the money moving through the platform, and control over payouts.

  • Monitor every order and its payment status
  • Approve or reject withdrawal requests
  • Track commissions earned on both sides

Reports & disputes

When a deal goes wrong, there is a queue and a process — not a dead end.

  • Review reports filed by buyers and sellers
  • Freeze funds while a dispute is investigated
  • Resolve, refund or release with a written reason

Marketplace analytics

Know what Bambili and Bamenda are actually buying and selling this month.

  • Listings, orders and revenue over time
  • Top categories and busiest neighborhoods
  • New signups and verification completion rate

Audit logs

Every administrative action is recorded, so moderation stays accountable.

  • Who changed what, and exactly when
  • Bans, deletions and refunds traced end to end
  • Exportable history for accountability
